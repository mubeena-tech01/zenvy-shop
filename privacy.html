Privacy Notice

Last Updated: 15th April 2026

At ZenvyShop, we believe in transparency. This notice describes how we collect, use, and protect your Personal Data in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

What We Collect

  • User Data: Your name, email, phone number, and birthday (for age verification).
  • Technical Data: IP address, device ID, browser type, and operating system information to optimize your experience.
  • Behavioral Data: Browsing history, page visits, and product interactions to provide personalized recommendations.
  • Purchase Data: Transaction details, order history, and body/fit measurements to assist with your purchases.

Why We Collect It

We use your data to:

  • Process your orders and manage returns or exchanges.
  • Send you important updates regarding your account or order status.
  • Improve our services based on your feedback and reviews.
  • Provide personalized ads and shopping recommendations tailored to your preferences.

Your Rights

You have the right to access your personal data, update your contact preferences, or request data deletion. You can manage these settings anytime through the "Privacy Settings" in your ZenvyShop account.
