Q&A

1. How can I track my order?
Go to My Orders section and click the relevant order for tracking.
2. How can I remove product from wishlist?
Open Wishlist and click remove button on the product card.
3. How do I change my address?
Go to Address Book from account menu, then add or edit address.
4. What's Zenvy Club?
Zenvy club is your membership for rewards, points and priority offers.
